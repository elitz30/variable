PRIVACY NOTE

Local by design.

Recognition runs in this browser. Face photos are converted to numerical descriptors; voice samples are converted to a numerical sound profile. Northstar saves those profiles plus the name you provide in this browser’s local database. It discards the photos and audio afterwards.

The public model files are downloaded when the app opens. Your camera frames, chosen photos, and microphone audio are not uploaded to the model host or to an app server.
